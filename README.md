> **One Health genomic surveillance reveals structured urban rabies transmission and major surveillance gaps**

**Authors**

Kirstyn Brunker¹²*, Alejandra Dávila-Barclay¹³, Elvis W. Díaz³, Sandeep Kasaragod¹², Rowan Durrant¹⁴, Katie Hampson¹, Edith Zegarra⁵, Ynés Monroy⁵, Ricardo Castillo-Neyra³⁶⁷*

## Repository contents

- `analysis/` – analysis workflows, phylogenetic and phylogeographic analyses
- `scripts/` – reusable analysis scripts
- `processed_data/` – processed datasets used in the analyses
- `raw_data/` – original data source
- `figures/` – figures generated for the manuscript
- `supplementary/` – supplementary materials

Large intermediate files (e.g. BEAST outputs, alignments, and GIS source files) are excluded from this repository.
Consensus genome sequences generated in this study are available on NCBI GenBank under BioProject: PRJNA1484877.

## Scripts
Scripts are organised by language (R, perl, python) and analysis component.

- `scripts/perl/collect_times` –  parses completeJumpHistory logs generated by Markov Jumps and Rewards, edited from original script available on https://beast.community/markov_jumps_rewards#references

- `scripts/python/add_tip_date.py` – adds sampling dates to phylogenetic tip metadata.

- `scripts/R/global-packages.R` – shared package loading and setup for R workflows.

- `scripts/R/contextual_analysis/` – contextual phylogenetic analyses on all LAC and subsequent AM5 subset, including tree and map visualisations:
  - `plot_contextual_map.R` – contextual map figures.
  - `plot_contextualTree.R` – contextual phylogenetic-tree figures.

- `scripts/R/discrete_trait_phylogeo/` – discrete-trait phylogeographic analyses of districts, river proximity, and urbanicity:
  - trait summary-table generation;
  - district downsampling and robustness analyses;
  - district, river, and urbanicity summary plots.

- `scripts/R/gen_dist/` – genetic-distance analyses:
  - `n167-genetic-distances.R` – calculation and analysis of genetic distances.

- `scripts/R/landscape_analysis/` – landscape-resistance and SERAPHIM diffusion analyses:
  - construction of accessibility, population, cropland, and other resistance layers;
  - implementation of SERAPHIM analyses and dispersal statistics;
  - resistance-layer maps and diffusion-result heatmaps/barplots.

- `scripts/R/map_tree_plots/` – manuscript map, phylogeny, and combined map–tree figures.

- `scripts/R/outbreak_skygrid_models/` – BEAST Skygrid analyses and estimated outbreak-case trajectories.

- `scripts/R/misc/` – script for extraction of gene alignments.

- `scripts/R/geo_cols.rds` and `scripts/R/phylogeny-country-cols.rds` – saved colour palettes used to ensure consistent geographic and phylogenetic visualisations.
